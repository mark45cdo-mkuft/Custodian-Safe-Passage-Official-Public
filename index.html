A calm human presence

Ordinary things shouldn't become impossible because you're alone.

Custodian Safe Passage is being developed to offer calm, bounded human support where an ordinary need is difficult to manage alone — whether that means accompaniment on a journey or appointment, help obtaining essential medicines or supplies through an approved route, or simply some planned company when isolation itself is the problem.

This is a pre-pilot service design. The details will continue to develop as professional review, safeguarding and delivery planning progress.

Proposed service shape

Simple for the person. Controlled underneath.

A future service request might be for accompaniment, an approved practical errand, or company. Whatever the form, the same basic discipline applies: define the need, check fit and safety, match the right volunteers, provide only the agreed support, then close or hand over cleanly.

1

Request privately

A person asks for a defined form of help through a controlled private route.

2

Check fit and safety

The request is assessed against the approved service scope, safeguarding requirements and safe delivery capacity.

3

Match an approved pair

The proposed default for in-person support is two eligible volunteers with only the information needed for that assignment.

4

Support and close

The team provides only the agreed help, then completes, refers or hands over the request through the appropriate route.

Before any pilot

Safeguarding comes before scale.

The project remains deliberately pre-pilot until the conditions needed for responsible delivery are closed for the activities actually offered.

Safeguarding ownership

Named responsibility, professional review and clear escalation routes.

Volunteer assurance

Identity, eligibility, vetting, training, conduct and removal procedures matched to the real role.

Insurance and legal review

Cover and legal structure appropriate to each activity actually offered.

Information protection

Data minimisation, secure communications, retention rules and controlled access.

Complaints and incidents

Clear reporting, safeguarding triage, review and proportionate restriction routes.

Bounded pilot

A small area, limited activity set and capacity, review point and explicit stop conditions before expansion.

Role clarity

Human support is the job.

Volunteers would provide a calm civilian presence around an accepted service request. Healthcare, pharmacy, social care, police, emergency, transport and other specialist services keep their own professional roles and powers.

Clear role, calm support.

Each request is considered against the support needed, safeguarding requirements and what the programme can safely provide. If another service is better placed to help, Safe Passage hands over rather than stretching the role.
